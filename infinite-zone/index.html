How an act is made

      
    
        
  1. The seed generates every zone: its name, time of day, palette, ground pattern, skyline and which sections it favors.
    2. 
        
  2. The seed and a segment index pick one production from that zone's mix.
    3. 
        
  3. The production writes terrain into 128×128 chunks with two collision layers, so loops work.
    4. 
        
  4. The generator tracks the speed you'll be carrying. Before any hazard it adds a climb, so every hazard is on screen at least half a second.
    5. 
        
  5. Falling into a gap drops you to a lower route with a spring back up. Early zones cost time, never a life.
    6. 
        
  6. Two test players gate every change: an expert bot that must clear 100 seeds, and a runner with human reaction time that must never die in the first two zones.
    7.
